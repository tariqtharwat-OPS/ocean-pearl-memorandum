Technical Model P026

SuperFrozen LCO2

Cryogenic thermal lock: Preserving biological structure at -60°C.

Thermal Momentum

Unlike conventional blast freezers that take 4-12 hours to reach -18°C, our LCO2 modules achieve a core temperature of -60°C in under 20 minutes. This bypasses the "Critical Zone" (0°C to -5°C) where cell-damaging ice crystals form.

Yield Protection

Zero drip-loss during defrost. By maintaining cellular integrity, the product retains its natural weight and texture, resulting in a 3-5% yield advantage over traditional methods.
